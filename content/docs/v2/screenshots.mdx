## Variables and modes

Open the **Variables** workspace to work with collections, mode values, and aliases. The showcase’s sample collection, **Fanta Brand**, uses Light and Dark values for the navy-and-orange launch card, plus spacing and radius tokens. **Semantic/Action** aliases **Color/Accent**. [Work with variables →](/docs/v2/concepts/variables) · [Official brand identity →](/docs/v2/brand)
