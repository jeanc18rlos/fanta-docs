Local design editing is free and does not consume hosted AI credits. Public
accounts receive **no trial, signup, or referral credits**. Hosted AI requires
paid credits; complimentary access is restricted to designated founder
accounts.

Buy subscriptions and one-time credit packs through
[web Billing](https://app.fantaisa.net/billing). Prices below are in USD before
applicable taxes. Billing shows available offers and final checkout terms.

## Subscriptions

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Monthly credits | Seats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | 0 | 1 |
| Pro | $24/month | $240/year | 3,000 | 1 |
| Plus | $49/month | $490/year | 7,000 | 1 |
| Max | $99/month | $990/year | 15,000 | 1 |
| Team | $40/month | $400/year | 6,000 shared | 5 |

Annual plans are paid once a year and release their credit allowance monthly,
not as a full-year grant at checkout. Pro, Plus, and Max are individual plans
with one seat and release 3,000, 7,000, and 15,000 credits per month respectively.
Team releases a shared 6,000 credits per month across its five seats. Allowances
require a verified paid term. Unused paid credits roll over. Larger individual
allowances do not promise unlimited generation or different model availability;
the app shows enabled models and each request's credit estimate.

## One-time credit packs

| Price | Credits |
|---|---|
| $5 | 500 |
| $12 | 1,500 |
| $35 | 5,000 |

Packs work with Free, Pro, Plus, Max, and Team. They add to the same
organization balance without starting a subscription. The organization owner or admin manages
purchases in web Billing. Credits arrive after verified payment; returning
from checkout alone does not credit the account.

## Companies and studios

Sign in with your Fanta account and
[create a company workspace](https://app.fantaisa.net/teams/new). Creating a
workspace does not start a subscription or grant free AI credits. Choose Team in that workspace's Billing page for five seats and
one shared paid balance; individual plans remain one-seat plans.

The workspace owner or admin can manage
[members and email invitations](https://app.fantaisa.net/organization) and
review [member usage](https://app.fantaisa.net/usage). Invitations require the
recipient to sign in with the verified email address that was invited. Select the intended workspace before buying:
Billing rechecks the workspace and asks you to reload if it has changed.

Share [project Git links](https://app.fantaisa.net/projects) through the
workspace while keeping repositories with your existing Git host. Each teammate still needs access at that host. These
links do not upload or host project files in Fanta.

To use a company balance in the desktop app, select the company workspace on
the web, then sign in again in Fanta on the desktop.

Pro, Plus, Max, and Team accounts are excluded from donated agent-trace
feedback storage and Fanta's training capture. This does not disable ordinary support or account
management. Connected AI providers apply their own data policies.

## Before running AI

The app shows the account's enabled models, estimated cost, and remaining
balance. One credit represents $0.01 in the AI pricing meter; subscriptions
and packs determine what you pay for the credit allowance. The current
in-app quote is authoritative for a new request.

**Requests blocked before provider dispatch and generation failures confirmed
during reconciliation bill 0 credits.** Interrupted requests can consume
credits, and uncertain provider results can keep credits reserved until
reconciliation. Prior successful metered agent calls retain their spend.

## Cancellation and partial refunds

Under our voluntary refund policy, **spent hosted AI credits are not
refundable**. Requests for partial refunds are limited to **unused paid
credits** and require manual review using the original purchase price and terms,
plus payment and usage evidence. Your current credit balance alone does not
determine refund eligibility or amount. Contact
[support@fantaisa.net](mailto:support@fantaisa.net) for review.

Canceling a subscription through web Billing stops its next renewal. Annual
cancellation does not automatically refund or prorate the annual payment.
Requests concerning unused credits or future annual allowances require manual
review; there are no automatic refunds or prorations. Deleting the Fanta account
does not cancel an external subscription.

Refunds required by law or the payment provider still apply. See
[Fanta's billing terms](https://www.fantaisa.net/terms#billing) and
[Polar's buyer terms](https://polar.sh/legal/checkout-buyer-terms).

The plan and pack catalog was checked **13 October 2026**. Model availability
and credit costs can change, so use the live catalog in the app rather than a
downloaded pricing snapshot. Local tools and supported provider keys are
separate from Fanta-hosted AI; connected providers apply their own charges.
