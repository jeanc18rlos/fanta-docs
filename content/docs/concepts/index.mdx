Fantaisa is a polymorphic infinite canvas: vector, bitmap, text, video, 3D, node graphs, and AI artifacts all share the same document semantics — transform, selection, grouping, undo, and export. Understanding a handful of core ideas makes both hand-editing and agent-driven design predictable.

These concepts are renderer-agnostic and tool-agnostic. They describe what a Fantaisa document *is*, independent of whether you draw with a mouse, drive the [editor](/docs/editor) by hand, or build over [MCP](/docs/mcp/best-output).

## How the pieces fit

A **document** holds **pages**, each placed in its own region of one shared **world space**. A page is a tree of **nodes** — frames, vectors, text, images — each with a globally-unique id. You change the document only through **Operations**, which the **history stack** records for undo and redo.

Layout, theming, and reuse sit on top of that tree. **Auto-layout** turns a frame into a flex container so children flow with consistent rhythm. **Variables** bind node properties to named tokens that resolve per mode. **Components** let one master drive many instances, with per-instance **overrides**. Vectors and icons render as resolution-independent paths. **Prototyping** adds interactivity, and **.fnx** lets the whole thing read and diff like source code.
