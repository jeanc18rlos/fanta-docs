---
title: Read, query & debug
description: Inspect the open project and page tree without mutating them.
---

`get_editor_state` reports the project root, pages, active page, selection, viewport, components, editability, and hints. Pass `empty_space: [width,height]` to find a free top-left for a new top-level frame.

`batch_get` without `ids` lists a page. It defaults to active page, depth 2, and 200 direct children. The result reports `child_count`, `children_offset`, `children_limit`, and `more_children`; continue with `offset` and `limit` until done. Pass exact `ids` for detailed nodes and `include_geometry: true` for world bounds. A result over 256 KiB is refused rather than truncated; narrow depth, child count, IDs, or geometry.

`read_fnx_source` without `path` lists source files. With a project-relative path, it returns source text with `truncated`, `next_offset`, and a notice. Follow those fields before making whole-file edits. Imported `.fig` pages may be much wider than one result.
