---
title: Auto-layout, transform & viewport
description: Use Fanta’s current layout and transform operations.
---

`set_auto_layout` takes an `id`, `direction` (`horizontal`, `vertical`, or `none`), and optional `gap`, `padding`, `align_items`, and `justify`. Padding accepts one, two, or four numbers. It also accepts `primary_sizing` and `counter_sizing` (`fixed` or `hug`), `min_size` and `max_size` as `[width,height]`, `wrap`, and `counter_gap` for wrapped rows. Use `null` in either size-limit slot to clear that axis's limit. Once a container is in auto-layout, change layout rules or sibling order instead of setting child `x` and `y`.

```json
{"label":"Lay out card","ops":[{"op":"set_auto_layout","id":"<frame-id>","direction":"vertical","gap":12,"padding":[20]}]}
```

Use `set_layout_child` on a child to set its positive `grow` value, cross-axis `align_self`, or `absolute` participation. A text node's `set_text_style.sizing` can be `fixed`, `auto_height` (wrap at its width), or `auto_width` (hug unwrapped text). For a minimum-size button, use horizontal layout, `hug` sizing, `[128,48]` minimum size, and an `auto_width` label. For a card, a fixed counter-axis width with `auto_height` body text gives predictable wrapping.

`align` aligns two or more nodes to a shared edge or center line, or one node to its parent. `distribute` spaces three or more nodes along an axis. `rotate` sets an absolute angle; `set_index` changes sibling z-order; `reparent` moves a node while preserving its world position. `set_viewport` stores canvas center or zoom. The alpha’s grid auto-layout control is not backed by the layout engine; use horizontal or vertical.
